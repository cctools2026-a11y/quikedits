# Get QuikEdits found when people search its name

## What we know
- The live site opens fine and Google is allowed to read every page.
- Semrush has no ranking data for quikedits.com yet. That usually means Google hasn't picked the site up, which is normal for a new domain.
- Google Search Console isn't connected, so we can't yet see whether Google has found or indexed the site.

## Likely reasons
1. The domain is new, and Google hasn't crawled it or decided to trust it yet. This can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks.
2. Google hasn't been told the site exists. Nothing has been submitted to Search Console.
3. Almost no other websites link to quikedits.com, so Google has little reason to find it.
4. "QuikEdits" looks like a misspelling of "quick edits", so Google may show results for that phrase instead until the brand is known.

## Steps
1. Connect Google Search Console for quikedits.com, verify that you own it, and submit the sitemap. Then ask Google to index the home page.
2. Check the home page and footer so the name "QuikEdits" appears clearly in the title, main heading and site details Google uses. Add details that tell Google the site is called QuikEdits and should be treated as a brand.
3. Get a few links to the site: your GitHub repo page, social profiles, and free listings on tool directories such as Product Hunt and AlternativeTo.
4. Check again in Search Console after 1–2 weeks.
